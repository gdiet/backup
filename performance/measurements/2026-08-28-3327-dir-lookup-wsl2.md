## Notes

First WSL2 lookup measurement on `3327` - closes the coverage gap `../overview.md` noted ("`3327`
has no WSL2 lookup row yet"). Run 1 is only ~5% below the median of runs 2-5, well under the
discard threshold - kept.

Roughly 2x `julius`'s own WSL2 result (104,351.4 vs. 50,350.2 ops/s, see
`2026-08-28-julius-dir-lookup-wsl2.md`), consistent with `3327`'s newer/faster CPU and NVMe vs.
SATA storage per `../machines.md`. Also, unlike `julius`, no step-change between early and late
runs here - all 5 runs land within a tight ~8% band, no sign of the scheduling/interference effect
`julius`'s own protocol flagged as unexplained.
