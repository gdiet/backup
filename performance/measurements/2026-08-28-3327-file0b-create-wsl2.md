## Notes

First WSL2 zero-byte-file-creation measurement on `3327` - closes a coverage gap (`../overview.md`
previously had a native-Windows row for this machine/operation but no WSL2 row). Run 1 (1001 ops/s)
is close to the median of runs 2-5 (~1048.5), not a warmup outlier - kept. Run 4 (941 ops/s) is the
one mild dip in an otherwise ~1000-1054 band; not investigated further, single-sample noise on a
non-isolated machine is the more likely explanation than anything systematic.

Roughly 1.5x `julius`'s own WSL2 result for this operation (1018.6 vs. 667.4 ops/s, see
`2026-08-27-julius-file0b-create-wsl2.md`) and close to this machine's own WSL2 directory-creation
throughput (984.6 ops/s, see `2026-08-28-3327-dir-create-wsl2.md`) - consistent with both
operations being dominated by the same per-call metadata-write cost on this filesystem, not by
anything content-specific (there is none for either).
